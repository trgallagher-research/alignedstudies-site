Report 1 · February 2026 · Complete

Benchmarking AI Models for Educational Practice

Five evaluations across 32 models from five providers, covering neuromyth identification, diagnostic reasoning, teacher certification knowledge, and judgement of student work. No single model ranks first on every task, and performance on one evaluation does not predict performance on another. On standards-based grading the best chance-adjusted agreement with experts was κ = 0.25.

Report 2 · March 2026 · Pilot

Can LLMs Assess Complex Student Competencies?

Two Gemini models scored 18 student essays against rubrics matched to each corpus, with expert scores as the reference. A contamination test found both models could recall published scores for the well-known ETS corpus without seeing the rubric. Assessment accuracy on public corpora may be inflated by score memorisation, which argues for contamination testing as standard practice.

Report 3 · March 2026 · Complete

AI and Education: What 152,000 Conversations Reveal

A descriptive analysis of the 152,088 education-related conversations in the Anthropic Economic Index V4. Students are the primary users, with 59.5% of education usage classified as coursework. Directive requests and task iteration dominate, while feedback loops are nearly absent at 1.6% of interactions.

Report 4.1 · March 2026 · Pilot

Do LLMs Fade Worked Examples?

Six models generated worked-example sequences under three prompting conditions. All six reference fading in their reasoning traces when told to apply cognitive load theory, yet none applies it in the actual output unless the prompt spells out what fading is. Extends an earlier two-model pilot to six models, including three open-weight models.
